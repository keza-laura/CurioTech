STAGE 1 OF 6

Discover the Secret of Web Pages

Before we write any code, let's explore where websites come from and how computers display them.

Think of the last time you opened a web browser—whether on your phone, tablet, or laptop—to search for game tips, listen to music, or watch videos.

In less than a second, an entire world appeared on your screen: bold headlines, photos, articles, menus, and clickable buttons.

A Question to Ponder:

How does the browser know where to place the title, which picture to show, or what should happen when you click a button?
