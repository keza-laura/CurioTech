STEP 4 OF 6

Run it, look at it, improve it

Creators try things, look, and try again. That is how every webpage gets better.

  • Press ▶ Run Code, then look at the preview and the Output box.
  • In the CSS tab, tap a styling chip, then change a colour yourself.
  • Change one small thing at a time and see what happens. You can't break anything!
  • Pick one thing you'd like to make better, and make it better.
Back to the Playground 🛠️
STEP 5 OF 6

Save your project

Your project only counts once you save it. Saving keeps it safe, even if you close the browser.

  1. Open the Creator's Playground and find the 💾 My Projects box.
  2. Type your project name in the box, then press Save Project.
  3. Come back here and press Check my saved project.

You already have saved projects? They stay safe. Press Save as New Project so you never replace one by accident.
