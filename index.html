About CurioTech

A beginner-friendly place to learn digital skills by building real things, made for young people in Kigali, with girls and women especially welcome.

  • The problem

    Learning technology can feel intimidating and too theoretical. Many beginners, and especially young women facing the stereotype that tech is only for men, give up before they build anything.

  • Our approach

    Short missions with Pixel, your robot guide. You discover an idea, take on a challenge, experiment, build, and create, with instant feedback at every step.

  • What you get

    XP, badges and a progress dashboard, plus the Creator's Playground where you write HTML, CSS and JavaScript and keep your own projects in a portfolio.

Why Learn With CurioTech?

Our learning approach is built around supportive, active participation.

  • Beginner-Friendly Learning

    Concepts are introduced without confusing jargon so you can start with confidence.

  • Practical Activities

    Bite-sized exercises help you test your understanding immediately after each lesson.

  • Learning Through Experimentation

    Try changes, see real-time results, and learn freely without fear of making mistakes.

  • Building Projects Step by Step

    Combine what you learn into creative mini-projects you can showcase with pride.
