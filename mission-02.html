Elements: the building blocks

A webpage is built from elements. Most elements have an opening tag, some content, and a closing tag.

<h1>Hello!</h1>
  • Opening tag <h1>: starts the element and says what kind it is.
  • Content Hello!: the words people see.
  • Closing tag </h1>: the same tag with a slash. It ends the element.

Every page also has a skeleton. The <head> holds information about the page, like its title in the browser tab. The <body> holds everything you can see.

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
  <head>
    <title>My Page</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Hello!</h1>
  </body>
</html>
